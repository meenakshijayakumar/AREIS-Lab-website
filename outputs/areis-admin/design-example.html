About the research

PollenMATIC is a handheld artificial pollination solution designed to provide reliable and controlled pollination in greenhouse crops. The device generates bee-like vibrations that promote pollen release and transfer, helping to improve pollination consistency while reducing dependence on natural pollinators and manual methods.
